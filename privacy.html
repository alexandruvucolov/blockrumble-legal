Privacy Policy for BlockRumble

Last updated: February 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BlockRumble ("the Game") handles information when you use the mobile application.

BlockRumble is designed to minimize data collection. The Game may process and store the following locally on your device:

Most gameplay data is stored locally on your device (for example, using local app storage such as PlayerPrefs). If you uninstall the app, local data may be removed.

The Game may rely on platform-level services (such as Google Play services, app store infrastructure, or Unity runtime components). These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

5. Children’s Privacy

BlockRumble is not directed specifically to children under the age required by local law for consent. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child provided personal data, please contact us.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not intentionally share personal data except where required by law or through essential platform services.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal information (access, deletion, correction, objection). Contact us to request support.
