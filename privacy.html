Privacy Policy for Block Rumble!

Last updated: February 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Block Rumble! ("the Game") handles information when you use the mobile application.

Block Rumble! is designed to minimize data collection. The Game may process and store the following locally on your device:

2. How Information Is Used

  • To provide core gameplay features and saved settings.
  • To improve stability and fix issues through diagnostics/crash reports (if available).

Most gameplay data is stored locally on your device (for example, using local app storage such as PlayerPrefs). If you uninstall the app, local data may be removed.

The Game may rely on platform-level services (such as Google Play services, app store infrastructure, or Unity runtime components). These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

AdMob's data collection and use are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You may opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings or visit Google Ad Settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Block Rumble! is not directed specifically to children under the age required by local law for consent. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child provided personal data, please contact us.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not intentionally share personal data except where required by law or through essential platform services.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Your Rights
